1. The blurting method, but faster

Rereading isn't studying. Blurting is: close your notes, write everything you remember, then check what you missed.

  1. Paste your notes and wrap the key terms in ###. They're hidden instantly.
  2. Tap Quiz and type each answer from memory.
  3. Tap Check Answers. Missed words are marked.
  4. Rate the review. Cairnote schedules the next one for you.

2. Stop making flashcards

Your class notes already are flashcards. Hide the definitions and test yourself straight away.

  1. Paste your notes and hide every definition with ###.
  2. Read the term, say the definition out loud, then tap the purple block to check.
  3. Tap + each time you get through it to log a round.
  4. Switch to Key Points to drill only what's hidden.

3. How to memorize diagrams

Cover the labels, not the picture. Works for anatomy, biology and maps.

  1. Add your diagram from Photos.
  2. Tap the image and draw a box over every label you need to know.
  3. Name each part, then tap the box to check.
  4. In Quiz, type the label for each numbered box.
