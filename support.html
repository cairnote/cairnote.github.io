常见问题

免费版和 Pro 有什么区别？

免费版可以创建 10 条笔记，所有功能都能用。订阅（月 / 年）或购买永久版后，笔记数量不限。已有笔记随时可以阅读、复习和导出。

换了手机，怎么找回购买？

用同一个 Apple 账户登录 App Store，打开 App →个人中心 →磊记 Pro →「恢复购买」。

我收到了兑换码，怎么用？

在 App 的订阅页点「兑换码」，或在 App Store 点头像 →「兑换充值卡或代码」输入。

怎么取消订阅？

iPhone「设置 → Apple 账户 → 订阅」中找到磊记，选择取消。当前周期结束前仍可正常使用。

笔记会丢吗？怎么备份？

笔记保存在你的手机上。建议定期在「个人中心 → 导出完整备份」保存一份到「文件」或电脑；也可以上传到你自己的 iCloud。

朗读的声音不好听，怎么换？

在 iPhone「设置 → 辅助功能 → 朗读内容 → 声音」下载你喜欢的语音，回到 App 后在朗读设置里选择即可。

FAQ

What’s the difference between Free and Pro?

The free plan includes 10 notes with every feature. With a monthly or yearly subscription or the Lifetime purchase, you get unlimited notes. You can always read, review and export the notes you already have.

I have a new phone. How do I get my purchase back?

Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account, then open the app → Profile → Cairnote Pro → Restore purchases.

How do I use a code I received?

Tap “Redeem code” on the subscription page, or in the App Store tap your photo → Redeem Gift Card or Code.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Go to Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions, choose Cairnote and cancel. You keep access until the current period ends.

Can I lose my notes? How do I back up?

Notes are stored on your iPhone. Export a full backup regularly from Profile → Export full backup and save it to Files or your computer, or upload to your own iCloud.

How do I get a nicer reading voice?

Download a voice you like in Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices, then choose it in the app’s read-aloud settings.
